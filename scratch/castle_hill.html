Local Design Considerations

What Castle Hill Projects Usually Involve

Most Castle Hill projects fall into a few types, and each has its own design and approval challenges. Here is how we approach them.

Knockdown Rebuilds and Custom Family Homes

When an older house no longer suits the family, rebuilding on the same block can deliver more than a patchwork of extensions. We test the site for what it can carry, then design a home with the right room mix, outlook and storage for the long term.

Second-Storey Additions

Building up keeps your garden and often your street. The work is as much structural and planning as it is design, so we check the existing footings, walls and setbacks early to avoid surprises once drawings are lodged.

Sloping Blocks and Bush-Edge Sites

Falls across a block, retaining walls and drainage affect cost and layout. Properties near bushland or reserves may also face bushfire requirements that influence materials, openings and setbacks. We investigate these constraints before the design is locked in.

Pools, Alfresco Areas and Outdoor Living

Larger blocks make generous outdoor spaces possible. We plan pools, covered alfresco areas and gardens together with the house, so the outdoor space connects properly to the kitchen and living areas rather than being added later.

Granny Flats and Secondary Dwellings

A granny flat can house extended family or provide rental income. We assess whether your lot suits one, how it sits alongside the main house, and which approval pathway applies.

Approvals Through The Hills Shire Council

Castle Hill projects are assessed against The Hills Shire Council's planning controls. We prepare the documentation, advise whether a Development Application (DA) or Complying Development Certificate (CDC) is the better route, and coordinate the consultants your approval needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle knockdown rebuilds in Castle Hill?

Yes. We assess what your block can support, design the new home, prepare the approval documentation and can coordinate construction through Daylo Build or your own builder.

Can you add a second storey to my Castle Hill home?

Often, yes, but it depends on the existing structure, your lot and the planning controls that apply. We check these early and advise whether a DA or CDC is the right pathway.

Can you design for a sloping or bushfire-prone block?

Yes. We design around falls, drainage and retaining needs, and consider bushfire requirements where a property backs onto bushland or reserves.

Can dp Design Studio design and build my project?

Yes. Daylo Build Pty Ltd, our licensed construction arm, can build from approved documentation. You can also take our drawings to another builder.
